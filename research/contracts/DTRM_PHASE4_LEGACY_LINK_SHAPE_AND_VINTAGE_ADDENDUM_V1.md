## Implications for future instrumentation

1. Current items are *candidate associations*, not independent assets or historically proven mapping edges; duplicate `ticker` across articles, duplicate edge within a single article, revision history and `match_type` semantics remain unknown. The presence of `match_type` string does not establish a confidence weight, score, correctness, mapping date, or issuer identity.
2. An experimental snapshot adapter must explicitly preserve the observed `ticker` and `match_type` **values privately and verbatim**, together with exact `content_version`, `mapping_version`, independent `version_observed_at` and `linked_at`, before any asset-level count is presented as point-in-time admissible. Do **not** coerce current string labels into scores or infer missing `confidence` as 1 or 0. The initial `match_type` vocabulary must be privately profiled under separately approved value access, then preregistered before any semantic normalization.
3. An edge's prospective `linked_at` must come from authenticated writer/matcher action and immutable stored receipt (or a separately valid provider observation that actually carries the edge), not from news `published_at`, Mongo ObjectId, latest document read time, GitHub dispatch schedule or the news version's `version_observed_at`.
4. When an old news document gets `matched_tickers` updated in place, the **new** edge receives its own prospective link vintage; prior snapshots must remain readable unchanged, and legacy edges without authenticated link history must be `UNKNOWN_LINK_CLOCK`, never backfilled from publication date. The independently observed `--apply` procedure proves a backend action occurred, but not document-level link first-seen.
5. Source coverage denominators count **distinct, frozen `(asset_id_asof,logical_event_id,admissible_version)`**, not raw elements. Deduplicate only using preregistered identity rules with immutable lineage; no asset inclusion/exclusion based on observed return or favorable history size.
6. For cutoffs, strict `max(version_observed_at,linked_at)<decision_cutoff_utc`. Equal, unknown, after-cutoff, imprecise, altered, unauthenticated or missing mapping authorization is unresolved and explicitly excluded from *positive* historical admission; `ZERO_AUTHENTICATED` requires independently proven complete coverage of registered lookback L.
7. Do not confuse weekday/weekend differences in *publication-date counts* with point-in-time asset-link availability or feed completeness. NYSE decision calendar and provider capture UTC cadence are separate clocks.
