## What is already a defensible contribution

1. Original V0 rank-replacement failure and V1 zero-admission failure were reported, not suppressed.
2. The original frozen ranking and q25/K10 veto produce positive **historical paired point differences** on beta-adjusted forward target in V2, V3, V4; updated G3.1 numerical precision correction preserves original point values.
3. G3.1 A2/A6 retrospective sensitivity demonstrates *policy dependence*, not optimum.
4. G3.2 A3 demonstrates that a conventional volatility screen can outperform P10 on same fixed ranking/K in old historical outcomes. That is a **falsification of universal P10 superiority**, not evidence that humans are useless.
5. Frozen protocol and code SHA hashes, fail-closed capacity and a declared adverse-result chain permit an unusually explicit audit of a constrained ranking experiment. Whether that audit mechanism itself constitutes a *novel major advance* remains open.

## Construct-validity bridge: measured vs asserted

**Measured:** constraints change selected Top-K rows and old beta-adjusted target summaries, hit rates, finite-sample resampling distributions under specified news/ticker schemes. Original target labels were already consumed in later sensitivity experiments.

**Not measured:** who holds authority to change AI mission, quality of human deliberation, user satisfaction, contestability, appeals/corrections, costs of human governance, regret relative to delegated autonomy, or user welfare. The distinction between *who sets goals* and *who optimizes them* can be a defensible design position without becoming a measured treatment.

**Therefore:** do not formulate 'there is little difference between machine alone and machine with humans controlling rules' as a finding. A no-significant-difference result would also not establish equivalence; preregistered noninferiority margin, power, randomization and appropriate confidence test are required in a future experiment.

## Claim-safe replacements, candidates for G5 (not final manuscript)

- **Current study question:** 'On a frozen machine-generated ranking, what changes when prespecified admissibility constraints are applied under a fixed selection budget, and how sensitive are these changes to policy choice and dependence-aware uncertainty?'
- **Core result:** 'In the previously observed historical cohorts, the original P10 filter improved the mean beta-adjusted outcome relative to the unfiltered ranking, while a conventional trailing-volatility filter had larger exploratory improvements. Results remain sensitive to uncertainty clustering, overlapping horizons, source vintage, and multiplicity.'
- **Architectural interpretation:** 'The study operationalizes one human-specified, deterministic constraint layer. It does not isolate a causal effect of human participation or measure the welfare effect of retaining decision authority.'
- **Future program:** 'Explicit human decision authority and autonomous decision making should be compared prospectively using predeclared utility, intervention and governance measures, and a substantively justified noninferiority margin.'
