## Purpose

The 60-calendar-day forward target (+5-day original price-alignment
tolerance) makes genuinely prospective late October 2026 observations
unavailable for full outcome evaluation by the 2026-11-25
revision deadline. A new event-time, retrospective
**outcome-blind** cohort is distinct from a prospective trial
and must have unused news with verified point-in-time features,
disjointness and a signed preregistration before any labels
are opened.

G1.3a inventories ONLY filenames, file sizes and date-like
tokens *in names* under two local original research roots.
It does not open any file contents: no Pickles, notebooks,
news text, embeddings, market prices, target outcomes or
model binaries. Filenames resembling targets/prices/returns
are excluded as positive source evidence. Filename dates
are not proof of actual event or collection timestamps.
