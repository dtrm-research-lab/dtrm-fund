## Claim boundaries for editorial revision

Historical reproduction supports a strong technical
*repeatability claim* concerning the original inputs,
predictions, calibration and guardrail decisions.
It does **not** supply new, temporally independent
evidence of incremental decision efficacy. The primary
outcome `forward_excess_beta` is **not realized portfolio
profit or trading P&L**. Cross-ticker dependence is a
material risk: prior ticker-cluster bootstrap intervals
for V2 and V4 incremental Top-K means include zero,
and the within-V4 weekly pattern was nonuniform.
Avoid claiming universal ticker/market-regime robustness
or statistically unqualified superiority.

A separate, appropriately precommitted unused event-date
cohort or genuinely prospective forward trial is planned
but currently **unpreregistered and unexecuted**. A
newly started 60-calendar-day trial cannot deliver a
fully matured realized target by the paper revision
deadline 2026-11-25. Distinguish retrospective
outcome-blind evaluation from true prospective tests.
