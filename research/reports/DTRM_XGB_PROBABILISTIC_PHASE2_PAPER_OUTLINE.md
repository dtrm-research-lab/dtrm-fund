## Claim boundary

Supported:
A frozen calibrated probabilistic downside guardrail produced
reproducible incremental decision improvement across three frozen
temporal tests.

Not supported:
Absolute profitability, uniform ticker-level robustness, invariant
weekly superiority, or fully independent trading opportunities.
